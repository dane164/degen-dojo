DOJO RULES

Three ways to play: Ranked Online (matched by ELO, random arena), VS CPU, or VS Friend on one keyboard. Best of three rounds, 60 seconds each. If time runs out, highest health takes the round.

MAX specialMeter holds 2 bars. At 2 bars, hold Block and press Special: 40% more damage.

Defence

  • Block takes a little chip damage and drains your blue guard bar. If it empties, you're dizzy and wide open.
  • Parry: tap block just before a hit lands. You take nothing, and the attacker's shield is locked for 1.5 seconds.
  • Chains and specials cancel on hit or block. Combos scale damage down.
  • Counter hit: tag them while they're starting an attack for 25% extra damage and a longer freeze.
  • Knocked down? Press any button to tech (quick rise), hold left/right to roll away or under them, or do nothing for a normal rise. You can't be hit while down or for a moment after standing.
  • Breaker: get knocked down 3 times in a few seconds and you wake up with a shockwave that shoves them off you.
