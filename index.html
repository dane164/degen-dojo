DOJO RULES

Pick a fighter, a CPU and an arena. Best of three rounds, 60 seconds each. If time runs out, highest health takes the round.

Controls

Move / jump / crouchA D · W · S (or arrows). Double-tap to dash.
PunchL-CLICK or J
KickR-CLICK or K
BlockHold SPACE. Hold S too for lows.
SpecialE, L or MID-CLICK when the meter is full. Hold to charge.

Lunge KickToward + Kick
Low Jab › SweepDown + Punch, Down + Kick
Dive KickIn the air: Down + Kick. Must be blocked standing.
ThrowBlock + Punch up close. Beats blocking.
Dash attackAttack during a dash

Defence

  • Block takes a little chip damage and drains your blue guard bar. If it empties, you're dizzy and wide open.
  • Parry: tap block just before a hit lands. No chip, the attacker staggers and you get meter.
  • Chains and specials can be cancelled on hit or block. Combos scale damage down.
