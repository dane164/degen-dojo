DOJO RULES

Pick your fighter, lock in, pick a CPU (or Random), pick an arena. Best of three rounds, 60 seconds each. Highest health wins if time runs out. Mirror matches allowed.

  • Move: A / D or arrows. Jump: W / ↑ (hold a direction to jump over). Crouch: S / ↓. Double-tap left/right to dash.
  • Punch: J (fast, short). Kick: K (slower, more reach). Hold ↓ for low attacks. Press in the air for jump attacks.
  • Block: hold Space. Hold ↓ + Space for low attacks; stand-block jump-ins. Blocked hits do a little chip damage.
  • Special: L when your meter is full. Meter fills by dealing and taking hits. Hold L while standing still to charge it, but you're wide open.
  • Pause: Esc or P. On mobile, use the buttons under the arena.

Whiffed attacks leave you open. The CPU punishes on Hard.
